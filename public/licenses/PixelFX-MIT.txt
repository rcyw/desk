Paper Bird sprite generated from the PixelFX critters/bird effect.
https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826/src/fx/critters/bird.js

Desk recolors the body, breast, and beak in lavender and packs 36 cropped
50 × 42 frames exported at 15 frames per second into a horizontal sheet.

Copy Pop sprite generated from the PixelFX pickups/collect-burst effect.
https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826/src/fx/pickups/collect-burst.js

Desk recolors 12 frames sampled from the late sparkles and packs them into
a horizontal sheet of 34 × 34 frames.
